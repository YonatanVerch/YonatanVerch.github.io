I am Yonatan Verch, a fourth-year B.Sc. Hons. Statistics student at York University with a strong passion for data, analytics, and emerging technologies. My academic journey has equipped me with a solid foundation in statistical analysis, data modeling, and data visualization, complemented by hands-on experience in Python, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, and Google Cloud platforms.

I have strengthened these skills through industry-recognized certifications, including Google AI Essentials, Google Business Intelligence Specialization, and Machine Learning on Google Cloud Specialization, applying concepts such as ETL workflows, feature engineering, model training, and MLOps to real-world projects. Notably, I developed CineFinder, an interactive movie discovery app built with React and the TMDB API, and completed data-driven analyses and dashboards that showcase practical applications of my statistical and business intelligence expertise.

Beyond academics, I actively volunteer with Humanity First, contributing to humanitarian initiatives emphasizing teamwork, organization, and community impact. At York University, I lead the Golden Social Club, fostering a welcoming and supportive environment for commuters, and I also tutor chess in my neighborhood, combining mentorship with analytical thinking. These experiences have strengthened my collaboration, leadership, and problem-solving skills.

I am passionate about applying my analytical and technical skills to real-world challenges while exploring creative pursuits such as photography, music, and content creation. I strive to combine rigorous data-driven thinking with creativity and adaptability to make meaningful contributions in both academic and professional settings.
